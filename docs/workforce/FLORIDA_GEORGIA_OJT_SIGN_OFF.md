# Florida and Georgia workforce OJT sign-off

Recorded: 2026-10-12.

## Scope

This record concerns workforce-funded on-the-job training (OJT), not school OJT, professional licensing, or approval of a training provider's entire curriculum. Approval of a training plan and verification of completed competencies are distinct steps. Local procedures determine the authorized staff and completion documentation; there is no specific statewide individual identified in this record.

## Florida

The local CareerSource workforce development board administers workforce-funded OJT. Florida's Workforce Policy 009 requires the OJT training plan to be agreed upon and signed by the participant, employer, and local workforce development board. It also requires local monitoring procedures to include validation of skills and competency attainment.

Contact the local CareerSource OJT/business services team to identify the authorized training-plan approver and the required competency-completion sign-off process.

Sources:
- [CareerSource Florida Workforce Policy 009: On-the-Job Training](https://careersourceflorida.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Workforce-Policy-009-On-the-Job-Training.pdf)
- [Florida standardized OJT agreement](https://floridajobs.org/docs/default-source/lwdb-resources/policy-and-guidance/final_ojt-agreement.pdf)

## Georgia

The local WorkSource workforce development area administers OJT with the employer. The Technical College System of Georgia Office of Workforce Development OJT resource guide describes local workforce development area involvement in participant eligibility and setting up OJT agreements.

Contact the local WorkSource OJT coordinator to confirm who is authorized to approve the training plan and who must sign competency/completion documentation. The cited guide does not establish a single statewide competency signatory. It is an older guide; confirm current local requirements before relying on it operationally.

Source:
- [Technical College System of Georgia OJT Resource Guide](https://www.tcsg.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/OJT-Resource-Guide_03.21_v2.pdf)
