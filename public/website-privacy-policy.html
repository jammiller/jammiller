Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy describes how DATAPULSE SOCIAL handles information when you visit datapulsesocial.com, use the social growth assessment, or contact us through the website. It is separate from the privacy policy for our mobile applications.

Information you provide

If you submit the social growth assessment, we collect the information you enter: your name, business name, email address, and any optional phone number, website, or industry details. We also receive your assessment answers, score, and result category. The assessment information is sent to our database provider, Supabase, so we can provide the result and follow up about our services.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you include in your message.

Cookies, local storage, and analytics

The cookie consent feature stores your choice in your browser's local storage for up to 180 days. When you accept analytics cookies, the site loads Google Analytics 4 from Google to measure website usage. The analytics configuration requests IP anonymization; Google may also receive technical and usage information through its service.

If you decline, the consent feature does not load Google Analytics. You can clear local storage or change your browser settings to remove or restrict locally stored information.

Third-party services

The site links to Google Calendar for appointment scheduling and Stripe for membership payments. If you follow those links, those providers handle information under their own privacy policies. Supabase processes assessment submissions on our behalf, and Google provides analytics when enabled by your consent.

How we use information

We use assessment submissions to generate the assessment result, respond to requests, and provide information about relevant services. We use analytics information, when enabled, to understand site usage and improve the website. We do not use the assessment answers to make automated decisions about eligibility for services.

Retention and your choices

We retain submitted information for as long as needed to respond to requests, provide services, and meet applicable obligations. You may contact us to ask about or request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable requirements.

Children's privacy

This business website is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Please do not submit children's personal information through the site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website practices change. The date above indicates when it was last revised.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact info@datapulsesocial.com.
