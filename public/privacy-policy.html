Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the PulseOS, Safety App, and StatsLab mobile applications ("the Apps") developed by DataPulse Social.

1. Data Collection

The Apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data entered into the Apps remains locally on your device.

2. PulseOS

  • Curriculum units, assessments, and analytics are stored only on your device
  • No student data is uploaded to any server
  • No account or login is required

3. Safety App

  • Emergency contacts and safety settings are stored only on your device
  • Location sharing is initiated locally and not transmitted to any server
  • No account or login is required

4. StatsLab

  • All datasets and statistical calculations are performed entirely on your device
  • No data is uploaded, shared, or transmitted
  • No account or login is required

5. Internet Access

The Apps require internet permission only for loading the application interface. No data is sent to external servers.

6. Third-Party Services

The Apps do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

7. Children's Privacy

The Apps do not collect any data from users of any age, including children. The Apps are safe for use by all age groups.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact
