## Safety App

**App name:** Safety App

**Short description (80 chars max):**
Personal safety tools for emergency contacts, SOS support, and location sharing.

**Full description (4000 chars max):**

Safety App is a personal safety companion designed to keep you connected to the people who matter most in an emergency. Store your emergency contacts, send SOS alerts, share your location with trusted contacts, and run safety check-ins -- all from one clean, focused app.

KEY FEATURES

Emergency Contacts
- Store your most important emergency contacts for quick access
- One-tap calling and messaging from the contacts screen
- Add custom labels (family, doctor, emergency services)

SOS Support
- Send an SOS alert to your emergency contacts with a single tap
- Includes your current location in the alert message
- Customizable SOS message text

Location Sharing
- Share your real-time location with trusted contacts
- Set a check-in timer so contacts are notified if you don't check back in time
- Location is shared only when you choose -- never automatically

Safety Check-Ins
- Schedule periodic check-ins
- If you miss a check-in, your contacts are automatically notified
- Perfect for hiking, traveling alone, or commuting

First Aid Reference
- Quick access to basic first aid instructions
- Covers common emergencies (CPR, choking, bleeding, burns)
- Step-by-step guidance designed to be readable under stress

WHO IT'S FOR
- Anyone who wants a personal safety tool on their phone
- Students walking home alone or across campus
- Hikers and outdoor enthusiasts
- People with medical conditions who want quick emergency contact access
- Parents who want their teens to have a safety tool

PRIVACY
- All contacts and settings are stored only on your device
- Location sharing is initiated by you and never runs in the background automatically
- No account or login required
- No ads, no tracking, no data selling

## StatsLab

**App name:** StatsLab

**Short description (80 chars max):**
Interactive statistics tool for datasets, descriptive stats, and inference.

**Full description (4000 chars max):**

StatsLab is a hands-on statistics laboratory for students, teachers, and anyone working with data. Explore real datasets, compute descriptive statistics, create visualizations, and run inference tests -- all on your device, no spreadsheet required.

KEY FEATURES

Datasets
- Six built-in datasets across education, health, psychology, biology, environment, and business
- Add your own custom data by typing or pasting numbers
- Switch between datasets instantly
- Each dataset includes a description and category tag

Descriptive Statistics
- Mean, median, mode, standard deviation, and variance
- Minimum, maximum, range, and sum
- Quartiles (Q1, Q2, Q3) for box plot analysis
- Skewness and kurtosis for distribution shape
- Sample size and complete summary in one view

Visualization
- Histograms with adjustable bin count
- Box plots for quartile visualization
- Normal distribution curve overlay
- Bar charts and line charts for trend analysis
- Clean, readable charts with navy and gold styling

Inference
- One-sample t-test with adjustable hypothesized mean
- T-statistic, degrees of freedom, and p-value
- Pearson correlation between two datasets
- Normal CDF and PDF calculations
- Results presented in plain language alongside the numbers

Learn
- Built-in reference explains each statistic
- Definitions and formulas for every measure
- Guidance on when to use each test
- Perfect for students learning statistics for the first time

WHO IT'S FOR
- Students in introductory statistics courses
- Teachers who need a demonstration tool for class
- Researchers who want quick descriptive stats on the go
- Anyone curious about data analysis

PRIVACY
- All calculations happen on your device
- No data is uploaded or shared
- No account required
- No ads, no tracking
